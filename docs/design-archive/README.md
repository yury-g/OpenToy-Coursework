The [current Plant v0.2](../../mechanical/plant-v02/README.md) and [Face v2 tour](../images/v2-face-tour.gif) are embedded in the same gallery using their existing repository files. Copies of the same animation from other checkpoints are not duplicated here.

All seven GIFs copied into this archive are the original animations, without re-encoding. Original notes and review snapshots retain their October 1 assumptions and limitations; this contribution does not refresh their motor/power research or qualify their printing and assembly. Historical draft STLs and G-code are not part of this visual archive. The current plant folder supplies its separate print-test parts.

For the four-concept and swan CAD, keep `servo-toy-library.scad` beside the model when opening it. All CAD and illustrations here are original project material under OpenToy's [MIT license](../../LICENSE). [checksums-sha256.json](checksums-sha256.json) records the archived files and their original source-relative filenames. The Blender scenes and predecessor print packages remain preserved in the original local project; this archive includes the editable OpenSCAD designs and finished visuals requested here.
