1. Identify & Orient RLCD

    Highlighted Clips & Features

      All 6 assembly steps
        STOP Rule — PLA & Electronics Safety Stop immediately if you feel hard resistance or see white PLA stress marks. Identify which highlighted tab or clip is making contact first. Never force the screen glass, pin headers, camera ribbon, or battery pouch.

        1. Board won't enter the opening

        • RLCD: Lay the housing flat on a soft cloth as shown. Remove the original acrylic stand legs first, but keep the protective rear acrylic backing. Screen glass faces down into the front bezel; enter from the open rear bay with the top button edge aligned to SNAP · PWR · BROWSE.
        • Camera Pod: Flip the pod straight up (`90°`). Remove all USB cables and the MicroSD card before sliding. Keep XIAO, Sense camera, and Expansion Base plugged together as one unit with the lens and small screen facing the stepped front faceplate.

        2. Stops just before fully seated

        • RLCD hang-up: Check whether the top button edge is tucked under the two rigid top tabs (do not bend these) before lowering the bottom edge past the four lower and side clips. Ease those four clips gently outward in the board plane—never pull away from the screen.
        • Pod hang-up near bottom: The two bottom mounting-hole detents click into the Expansion Base corner holes. To ease seating or release, pull their side finger tabs gently away from the circuit board toward the back wall.

        3. Lens or small screen window mismatch

        • Do NOT rotate the board 180° or force it. Confirm the stepped front faceplate matches the camera lens side and small OLED screen side, and check that cables are disconnected.
        • Hardware revision caveat: The 3D model uses an approximate vendor envelope (the actual firmware camera sensor is OV2640, and exact lens variant, header height, or RLCD revision may differ).
        CAD Validation vs Real Physical Fit Digital checks prove rigid geometry clearance on vendor envelopes—not physical PLA spring deflection, layer friction, or your exact board tolerances.
