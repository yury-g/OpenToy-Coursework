COMPACT CYD S-FOLD — A2 CASE REVISION
2026-11-24 • Three ESP32-2432S028 boards, 86 x 50 mm PCB

WHAT CHANGED
The case uses 54 mm board pitch and 53.2 mm face frames, leaving 0.8 mm plastic
seams. A common dark bezel masks the yellow boards. It cannot make three LCDs
physically seamless: each active display is 43.2 mm wide, so active image gaps
are 10.8 mm at this pitch. The reference active height is 57.6 mm. The viewer
uses the full 50 x 69 mm glass/module envelope and a 44 x 58.4 mm bezel opening.
The active area's longitudinal center (-2.5 mm) is inferred from reference CAD.

No long projecting hinge arms remain. Captive bearings sit in the bottom bezel
bay, within the overall rectangular footprint. Small rounded bearing portions
remain visible at the bottom/oblique views; this is not a fully flush concealed
hinge. Both race ridges per seam are clustered in that bay. Torsional stiffness
of the far/top ends of the panels has not been measured.

The A hinge remains Yury's selected earlier sample. This compact revision uses
B-derived annular dovetail races because the new upright build orientation and
front/back S-fold axes need a different self-supporting bearing construction.
It is a new mechanical prototype, not a validated cosmetic change to A.

WHAT TO PRINT
The arranged 3MF has four objects: one assembled upright chassis containing
three captive moving bodies, plus three flat face frames. Keep the orientation
and the three chassis bodies together. No loose hinge pins or hinge assembly
are needed. The separate face frames snap on after the boards are inserted.
They print flat to avoid an unsupported 44 mm window bridge in the upright case.

'Chassis - print upright.stl' is already in build orientation. The open-view
left/middle/right STLs, 'Chassis - open inspection.stl', and assembled bezel
meshes are for inspection, not separate print-and-assemble chassis parts.
The three files ending 'bezel - print flat.stl' are the printable face frames.

Use PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and first layer, three walls, four top and
bottom layers, 15% infill, 100% scale and supports off. The race ramps are 45°;
the fixed spoke bridges approximately 3.8 mm. The lower USB-back relief has a
45° peaked closure; the upper opening stays open. Snap features include thin
beams and small latch ledges: inspect them in the slicer and use a wall generator
that preserves thin features. Actual adhesion, bridge sag and release are not
verified. Do not automatically lay the chassis flat or add supports in bearings.

BUILD ENVELOPES
Unfolded assembly including bearings: about 161.2 x 107 x 27.7 mm.
Folded assembly including face frames: about 64.4 x 107 x 49.2 mm.
Upright chassis print: about 161.2 x 27.7 mm footprint, 107 mm tall.
The front-side bearing makes the lower end thicker than the screen region.

BOARD INSTALLATION AND USB
Unplug the boards. Place each PCB onto its four corner seats, with the display
facing the frame opening. Press the correct face frame into place by its bare
plastic edges, avoiding glass pressure. Four underside stops per frame contact
bare PCB corners after nominal 0.2 mm lift. The frame is 0.5 mm clear of the
reference glass when seated. Side spring legs latch into the chassis. Physical
snap force and exact board tolerances still need a fit trial. The left and
middle frames have a different bottom-edge notch from the right frame; retain
their labels/file identities.

Short ends and 40 mm wide back reliefs provide USB approach space. Connector
revision, overmould size and fitted headers are not measured. The central rear
component envelope in the checks is 40 x 70 x 5.9 mm; it does not replace an
exact component model. Verify your populated board and cable before installing
all three. Unplug USB cables before folding; connected-cable folding is not
validated. The design still uses external USB power, one cable per board.

FOLD
Start with all screens forward. Fold the LEFT screen forward over the middle;
then fold the RIGHT screen backward behind the middle. Unfold in reverse order.
The left axis is X=-27, Z=16; the right axis is X=27, Z=-1.3. Both rotations are
+180° around global +Y in the supplied open-coordinate meshes. This is an S fold.
There are no positive angle stops, friction-holding feature or travel latch.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
The final CAD has three watertight chassis bodies and three watertight face
frames. A 362-pose staged 0–180° check found no shell intersections and only
floating-point noise against PCB/glass/rear-component proxy envelopes. Seated
frame clips have zero solid overlap; raising a frame 0.5 mm intersects its latch
ledges. Rigid axial bearing displacement of +/-0.7 mm intersects retention
flanks. These checks do not establish all-path extraction resistance, holding
torque, loaded stiffness, wear, print release, snap life, or exact USB fit.
Nothing has been sliced or physically printed for this revision.
