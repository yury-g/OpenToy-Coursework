ACCESS DESIGN BASIS
Each confirmed board has USB-C AND micro-USB. Each panel has a largely open rear
and long side openings for buttons, headers, speaker connector and microSD.
PCB underside datum is Z12 mm, with upper corner stops0.2 mm above PCB top. The
face frame clears the full50 x69 mm glass/module proxy; its underside is1.35 mm

Both USB approach envelopes were checked on every board at X offsets+/-10 mm:
18 mm wide x11 mm high x22 mm insertion corridor, followed by a conservative
6 x6 mm cable corridor extending beyond the end of the case. These are generous
illustrative envelopes, not measured connector locations for this exact dual-USB
revision. The two overmould paths were checked independently. Side connectors
may need the neighbor folded away; a75-degree service pose was checked with
illustrative IO/DHT, microSD, speaker and BOOT/RESET access volumes. Exact plugs,
buttons, microSD and added headers still need a physical fit check. The reference
interface photograph predates the user's dual-USB revision.
