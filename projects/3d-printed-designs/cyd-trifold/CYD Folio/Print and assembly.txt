CYD FOLDING FOLIO  •  Prototype 1.1 · USB cable clearance revision
Three portrait screens, one attached cover and a removable travel clip.

START HERE

Check the marking on the back of each board. The reference is the 2.8-inch
ESP32-2432S028R, with an 86 × 50 mm PCB and four corner holes on a 78 × 42 mm
pattern. If a board has a different marking, a second USB connector or headers
installed, use the board fit gauge first. Even boards sold under the same name
can differ. The reference has a 1.5 mm PCB and a 5.1 mm total height from the
back of the PCB to the top of the glass. Rear components have 5.9 mm of space
above the inside back wall, except where the corner seats support the PCB.

PRINT

Use PETG, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, three walls and 15% infill. Keep
the scale at 100%. All STL files are in millimetres and already oriented.
Turn off supports, raft, brim and auto-orientation. Import the folio as a
single object with its four moving shells in their supplied positions. Its
three hinge pins print inside their barrels. The angled layout is intentional.

Times include startup and are estimates for separate plates using Bambu
Studio's A1 mini and Generic PETG profiles on textured PEI. Select your own
printer and material profile. Body build envelope is 145 × 154 × 91 mm.
Allow additional bed margin for your machine's purge line and other exclusions.

1. Print the hinge test. After it cools, hold the two tabs close to the pin
   and gently rock them. The tabs should move independently and stay joined.
   If fused, check for first-layer spreading or PETG strings. In OpenSCAD,
   raise hinge_radial_gap from 0.40 to 0.50 mm and, if the flat knuckle ends
   stick, hinge_axial_gap from 0.40 to 0.60 mm. Re-export and retry the test
   before exporting the body with the same settings.
2. Try one board in the fit gauge using the mounting steps below. Check that
   the clips clear the glass, the board stays seated, and your USB plug can
   fully engage. The gauge checks the corner fit and port approach. Check
   tall components against the 5.9 mm rear clearance separately.
3. Print the body and travel clip. After cooling, free the three hinges one
   at a time, supporting the printed edges next to each hinge. Rehearse the
   folding sequence with the empty case before installing electronics.

MOUNT THE BOARDS

Unplug the board. With the display facing you, tuck the two bare corners of
one short edge under the fixed lower lips. Put the USB short edge toward the
opposite, spring-clip end. Tilt the board down onto the four seats. Press
each upper bare PCB corner until its spring tab catches. Apply pressure only
to the PCB. The glass should sit below the protective long rails. To remove
a board, ease one upper tab outward, lift that PCB corner slightly, repeat
at the other corner, then slide the lower end free.

All three boards have independent USB access through the open short ends.
The cable end has a 42 mm wide × 9.5 mm deep relief in the back wall. A solid
18 × 11 mm plug housing cleared all three trays at the reference Micro-USB
location and the estimated CYD2USB port locations in the open assembly. The
two-port positions were inferred from the CYD2USB Enclosure Bottom port
cutouts, Printables model 840199. Try your exact plug in the supplied gauge.

One powered USB hub or multiport 5 V supply can serve all three boards, with
one cable per board. Separate LiPo packs are unnecessary. Some CYD2USB boards
require USB-A to USB-C cables for power from USB-A ports. See the CYD community
notes at https://github.com/witnessmenow/ESP32-Cheap-Yellow-Display/blob/main/cyd.md

The design assumes external USB power. Install a microSD card before seating
the board if your revision places the slot behind a side rail.

FOLD AND LATCH

1. Unplug the cables and open all three panels with their screens facing you.
2. Fold the outer screen without the lid face-to-face onto the middle screen.
3. Swing the lid over its own screen. Fold that covered pair behind the
   middle screen. Turn the stack so the solid cover faces up.
4. Slide the travel clip onto the centre of the short edge with the two
   matching grooves. Its arms span the solid lid and the opposite outer back.
   A gentle click holds the stack closed. Slide the clip off to unfold.

Closed body is 91 × 78.5 × 52.2 mm including the hinge barrels, before the
travel clip. Fully flat width is 271 mm including the open lid. The hinges
swing freely. Lay the folio on a table or angle its wings for an upright pose.

The enclosure geometry is in CYD Folio.scad. The reference board in the
preview is for illustration. The exported body contains four watertight parts.
The three seated board positions, the seated closure clip, and a sampled
five-degree folding sequence passed digital interference checks. All four
STLs sliced in Bambu Studio. Physical fit, hinge release and clip force still
need a first print.
