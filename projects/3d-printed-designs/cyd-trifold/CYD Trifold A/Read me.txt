CYD TRIFOLD A — THREE SCREENS, ONE PRINT-IN-PLACE HOLDER
2026-11-24 • Based on Yury's preferred inward-cone hinge A

This prototype holds three ESP32-2432S028 boards (86 x 50 mm PCB) in portrait,
side by side. It unfolds flat and folds sequentially into a three-layer stack.
The short opposed-cone bearings inherit A's nominal geometry and clearances.
There are two bearing units on each seam, four units total, with no loose pins
or hinge assembly. Three complete trays print already captive.

FOLDING
With all screens facing you, fold the LEFT screen face-to-face onto the middle.
Then fold the RIGHT screen over that pair. Unfold in reverse order. The unequal
axis heights accommodate the tray thickness: left axis Z15, right axis Z23.
This is an inward roll fold, not an alternating S fold. Do not force the other
fold direction. No hard stop, holding-torque feature or travel latch is included.
The screen-to-screen seam is visible: tray pitch70, PCB width50 (20 mm PCB gap).
Plastic tray edges have a 12 mm gap; short hinges sit beyond the tray ends.

PRINT
Use the assembled 'Trifold A - print flat.stl' or the supplied arranged 3MF.
Do not split/auto-arrange its three captive bodies. Units mm, scale100%, as
supplied flat. U1 270x270 bed; intended PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and
first layer, three walls, four top/bottom layers, 15% infill. Supports/raft/brim
off. Use your calibrated PLA cooling and flow. The cones use nominal45-degree
flanks. Tall supports and small snap lips still need a physical print trial.
The component STLs and board proxies are inspection/viewer files, not separate
parts to print and assemble. No slice or print has been started for this design.

BOARD FIT AND USB
Print 'Board fit tray.stl' first if you have not tested the revised tray fit.
The PCB sits at Z8, with 6 mm nominal room above the 2 mm back plate. Glass proxy
reaches Z13.1, below protective side rails at14.2. Four corner pads support the
bare PCB, and four flexible tabs retain its exposed long edges. Ease the tabs
outward and seat/remove the unplugged board by its bare PCB corners; do not
push on the glass. Snap force, fatigue and loaded retention are not tested.

Both short ends remain broadly open for USB; the back has a 40 mm wide cable
relief at each end. USB connector position, cable overmould and fitted headers
have not been measured. The reference screen and rear-component blocks in the
viewer illustrate clearance assumptions, not exact board-component geometry.
All three boards can use external USB power individually. Unplug cables before
folding; no cable routing through the hinges or connected-cable fold is proven.

WHAT IS CHECKED
See metadata.json for final bounds and digital collision checks of the staged
0–180° sequence, including PCB, glass and central rear-component proxy boxes.
These do not establish physical print release, durability, all-path retention,
USB-plug fit or exact component fit. A was selected as best by the user, but
physical test conditions were not provided. This larger, raised-axis version
needs its own print trial. Source is 'Trifold A.scad'; inspection components
are left.stl, middle.stl, right.stl. Keep selected A's .40 mm normal cone gap
and .50 mm axial face gap unless trial evidence calls for a controlled change.
