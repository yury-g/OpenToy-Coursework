FOUR ROUNDED PRINT-IN-PLACE MINIATURE NOTEBOOKS
Created 2026-11-24 for a Snapmaker U1

Open START HERE - Rounded notebooks.3mf in Snapmaker Orca. It contains four
notebooks already positioned on one plate. Each notebook has two hinge units
joining its two panels, and each is a separate object containing both captive
bodies. Preserve each object's internal placement and the supplied orientation.
Choose the U1 0.4 mm nozzle preset, select your PLA, slice, and inspect the preview.

A — Baseline design, adapted into a twin-hinge notebook in this parent chat.
    Inward opposed cones, 0.40 mm normal clearance. A small underside
    protrusion was trimmed so the broad panel feet start on the bed.

B — Astra 6.2. Upright notebook with two annular dovetail races on its spine.
    The ring shoulders grow at 45 degrees. Its overhead spoke is a short bridge.

C — Sol 6.2. Flat notebook with outward cones, through-vented sockets,
    0.50 mm normal clearance and relief at the first-layer seam.

D — Astra 6. Flat notebook with pointed outward cones in blind sockets,
    0.45 mm normal clearance and relieved bottom edges.

Each notebook carries its letter, creation date and model attribution.
Each model chose rounded panel corners and softened exposed hinge edges.
Mating bearing surfaces retain their defined profiles and clearances.
The models chose their own designs and panel details. This compares complete
designs; multiple geometric differences change at once.

SHARED PLA STARTING SETTINGS
Use one PLA spool and one toolhead for all four so material changes do not
obscure the comparison. Assumed nozzle is the U1 standard 0.4 mm.
Layer height 0.20 mm; first layer 0.20 mm; three walls; four top/bottom layers;
15% infill; 100% scale; print all objects by layer.
Supports off. Brim and raft off. Preserve the supplied print orientations.
The broad contact areas on A/C/D and the upright B are intentional.
Start with your calibrated PLA temperature and cooling profile. Suggested
conservative limits are 40 mm/s outer walls, 60 mm/s inner walls and
20–25 mm/s bridges/steep overhangs. Enable the profile's overhang cooling.
Keep a clean bed and avoid excess first-layer squish. First-layer spread and
stringing can fuse moving parts even when the digital model has clearance.

FIRST PHYSICAL COMPARISON
Cool the plate fully and remove each notebook as a complete assembly.
Free incidental strings gently, holding near the hinge rather than levering
hard on the panel. No pins, screws or assembly operations are required.
Try each at 0, 45, 90 and 180 degrees in the viewer's indicated direction.
Compare release effort, bearing play, smoothness, whether the two hinges bind,
and whether it stays captive. Open and close it 20 times and compare again.
A fused, cracked or loose sample is useful evidence. Keep the labels visible.

The meshes and folding motion are checked digitally. Actual release, wear,
strength and lettering quality remain the purpose of this PLA trial.
The 3MF is an arranged geometry file. Choose your printer and filament in
your slicer. You control slicing and the physical print.

Open Compare notebooks.html locally to inspect the whole plate, or select
A/B/C/D to orbit, fold, explode and section an individual notebook.
