A6 — FACE-DOWN, NEAR-FLUSH CYD TRIFOLD
2026-11-25

ONE flat print with THREE captive moving frames. No separate hinge pins,
cartridges, bezels or case panels. Install only the three CYD boards.

The boards are actually remounted face-down, rotated 180 degrees about their
long Y axis. USB stays at the +Y end; left/right interfaces swap sides.
In the flat print pose, all screens face -Z. The electronics and four
integrated A hinges are on the +Z back. This is not a flipped camera view.

NEAR-FLUSH FRONT
The front frame plane is Z0. All three glass faces are at Z0.4: a 0.4 mm
protective recess. Every frame has a full 50.8 x 69.8 mm clear glass aperture;
there are no ribs, grids or hinge feet crossing the glass or active image.
PCB screen-side seats are Z4.0; PCB occupies Z4.0..5.5, full glass Z0.4..4.0,
and the conservative rear-component envelope Z5.5..11.4.

The USB clearance is a shallow top-open notch at the end band. Its local
floor remains 1.6 mm thick, with a sloped inner transition, while a 5.6 mm
full-depth inner crossband and the corner/side routes remain 3 mm thick.
There is no roof or suspended bridge over the USB plug path.

The board pitch is 68 mm. This is 4 mm wider than A5 to preserve approximately
3.5 mm perimeter ribs beside the full-width glass aperture and bearing
sweeps. The projected tray-edge seam is 10 mm, and the gap between the
43.2 mm active image widths is 24.8 mm. Three distinct LCDs remain visible.
Open envelope: 194 x 99 x 27.35 mm. Closed: 76.8 x 99 x 46.0 mm.

A HINGES AND STRUCTURE
A's opposed cone/divot contact geometry is retained: 3.35 mm cone radius,
2.85 mm depth, 0.40 mm normal clearance and 0.50 mm axial clearance. Supporting
cheeks are taller than the original study and narrowed outside the bearing
lands; relief windows remove low-load material. The chassis is mirrored in X
to preserve the connector corridors after reversing the boards.

Two hinge units per seam have centers Y +/-30.5, spanning Y +/-43.5. They sit
along the boards instead of far beyond their ends. Broad end bands and the
perimeter frame carry the loads around the glass. Bearing posts/walls are
3 mm; the clipped male-foot strip is at least 3.1 mm, backed by the frame rim.
The frame remains open at the back and sides for components and access.

CAD volume: 38.82 cm 3. The same A6 geometry without optional bearing-support
reliefs uses 41.37 cm 3; those reliefs remove 6.14%. Mandatory glass and USB
openings remain in both versions. Total CAD volume is 36.6% below A5, which
had a different face-up back-frame structure. Solid-equivalent mass is
48.14 g at assumed PLA density 1.24 g/cm 3. Actual slicer mass is profile-dependent.

PRINTING
Print the arranged A6 3MF or One-piece trifold - print flat.stl at 100% scale
in the supplied orientation. Keep all three captive bodies together as one
object. Individual left/middle/right STLs are inspection/viewer assets.
All three frames contact the bed. Mesh checks found no meaningful elevated
horizontal underside or downward facet beyond 45 degrees from vertical.
Supports and raft are unnecessary in the intended geometry; keep any brim
out of moving gaps. The original A clearance assumptions used a 0.4 mm nozzle
and 0.20 mm layer. Select the actual installed nozzle; no profile was changed.
Check the sliced gaps and flexures before printing. Physical release and fit
are still untested for this loaded-holder adaptation.

INSTALLING BOARDS
Work from the open electronics side, with displays pointing through the
front apertures. Tuck the USB end's bare PCB corners under the fixed lips.
Hold both protected catches at the other end back slightly, lower the board
onto its four corner seats, then release. The checked insertion tilt is
0–10 degrees about the USB end. Press on bare PCB corners, not the display.
Reverse for removal. Release/retaining force requires a physical trial.

Each short catch is 5.2 mm wide, on a horizontal 2 x 3 mm beam with about 17 mm
free span. The rear travel stop is 0.9 mm away and side guards leave 0.8 mm.
The root-cut check confirms the two flexures are not fused into bearing feet.
Rigid released-catch and tilted-board checks include the full glass sweep;
these are geometric tests, not elastic force or fatigue simulations.

CONNECTORS AND BUTTONS
USB-C and micro-USB access on each board was checked using 18 x 11 mm plug
proxies, 22 mm insertion depth at X offsets +/-10, and a 6 x 6 mm cable path
continuing beyond the frame. The adjacent power connection shares the open
short-end region. Exact connector positions/plug overmoulds remain unmeasured
for this dual-USB revision; these are design envelopes.

For GPIO/extended IO, DHT, microSD and speaker insertion on inner edges, use
the service pose: right -90 degrees, left +90, middle fixed. Both the pose
and its loaded approach were checked. Button probes now approach from the
open +Z electronics side; approximate mirrored RESET/BOOT positions clear
separate 3 mm blunt-probe paths. The checks do not imply measured fingertip
clearance. Folding with attached plugs or protruding cards is not modeled.

FOLDING
In the supplied screen-down pose, fold the RIGHT wing upward over the middle
first, then the LEFT over the pair. Unfold in reverse order. Right pivot:
X34, Z16.8, rotation -180 degrees. Left pivot: X-34, Z23, rotation +180 degrees.
Both axes run along Y. Use the viewer's right-first sequence if you turn the
whole object over; apparent left/right changes when viewed from the front.
No closing latch, friction-holding function or detent is claimed. Some glass
remains on the outside of the folded stack; this is not a protective cover.

VERIFICATION

checks, full-glass front sight volumes, six USB/cable corridors, 21 mirrored
