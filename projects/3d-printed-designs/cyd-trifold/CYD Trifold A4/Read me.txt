A4 — ONE PRINT-IN-PLACE CYD TRIFOLD
2026-11-24

Print the complete chassis as ONE object with its THREE captive moving bodies
kept in the supplied positions. No cartridges, hinge pins, separate bezels or
case panels are assembled afterward. Install only the three boards.

This revision restores the A opposed-cone/divot mechanism. Cone radius3.35 mm,
cone depth2.85 mm, normal clearance0.40 mm and axial clearance0.50 mm are retained.
The supports are taller and bed-rooted to clear the loaded trays when folded;
non-bearing windows lighten them. There are two short units per fold, at the
top and bottom, four units total. It is a new loaded-holder adaptation of A;
physical strength, release and fit are not established by the earlier sample.

PRINT FLAT
Use the arranged A4 plate3MF or One-piece trifold - print flat.stl. The three
individual panel meshes are viewer/inspection assets, not parts to print and
assemble. Keep100% scale and the flat supplied orientation. Supports and raft
off; keep any brim out of moving gaps. Select your actual installed nozzle in
the slicer. The project geometry uses the original0.4 mm nozzle /0.20 mm layer
clearance assumptions; no printer profile was changed. Inspect the sliced
bearing clearances and horizontal flexures before printing. Use enough walls
or local solid fill to make the flexures and bearing walls substantial with
your chosen nozzle. Physical release, spring force and strength need a trial.

All three shells contact the bed. Final mesh analysis found no elevated

The flexure beams lie directly on the bed and remain joined at their roots.

BOARD INSTALLATION — NO CASE ASSEMBLY
The trays are for the confirmed86 x50 mm dual USB-C + micro-USB CYD boards. With
power unplugged, tuck the USB end's two bare PCB corners under the fixed lips.
Raise the opposite end only about10 degrees. Hold the two protected catches at
the non-USB end back slightly, lower the board onto all four seats, then release
the catches. Apply force to bare PCB corners, not glass. To remove a board,
release one catch and then the other, lift that end, and slide the USB end free.
Do not force a binding catch. Actual snap/release force remains untested.

Each catch is a broad5.2 mm block supported by a horizontal2 x3 mm PLA beam.
Its effective free span is about17 mm between the rounded root and catch. Rear
stops limit nominal motion to0.9 mm; side guards leave0.8 mm clearance. The
CAD root-cut test separated both catch/beam sections from the body, confirming
that neither was accidentally fused to a hinge foot or support. Released-catch
clearance and board insertion were checked geometrically, not by force or
elastic-strain simulation.

CONNECTORS AND BUTTONS
Raised PCB seats and the solid3 mm USB-end load band keep both USB approaches
clear while strengthening the positive-end hinge connection. Each USB check
uses an18 x11 mm overmould with22 mm insertion depth at X offsets+/-10 mm, then
a6 x6 mm cable corridor continuing beyond the case. These are design envelopes,
not measurements of the user's exact dual-USB connector positions. The power
connector shares the generous short-end access region. Rear windows and local
side openings provide access to BOOT, RESET, GPIO/extended IO, DHT, speaker and
microSD. For large inner-edge plugs or card insertion, angle the neighboring
panel away; the viewer's service pose illustrates this. Unplug cables before
folding and account for any protruding card or added wiring.

FOLD
Start with all screens facing you. Fold the LEFT screen forward over the middle,
then the RIGHT over the pair. Unfold in reverse order. This is an inward roll
fold, not the rejected modular S-fold. Left axis: X=-32, Z18.3; right axis:
X32, Z28.3. Both axes run along Y. The right panel rotates-180 degrees; left+180.
There is no travel latch, detent or designed friction-holding feature.

The64 mm board pitch gives6 mm between58 mm tray edges and20.8 mm between43.2 mm
active images. A60 mm pitch passed folding but interfered with USB housings;
64 mm retains lateral plug clearance without thinning the bearing supports.
