CYD folio revision 2 — one-fold print trial

Print 01 - Two-panel cone hinge.stl first. It is a small mechanism sample,
52 × 26 × 8.65 mm, designed to test the joint before extending the design
to three board trays. The case cover has been dropped from the revision.

Two short opposed cones, integral to the fixed cheeks, are captured inside
two sockets in the other half. This adapts the cone bearing arrangement
from the November 23 CrowPanel folding enclosure stand. Each cone projects
2.85 mm and tapers at 45 degrees, with 0.40 mm nominal normal clearance and
a 0.50 mm gap between the cheek and socket body. The cones and sockets print
together. There is no shaft to insert.

Print flat as supplied, at 100% scale. Keep the two captive bodies in place
as one object. Suggested first trial uses PLA, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm
layers, three walls and 15% infill. Supports, raft and brim are off. All
parts have feet on the plate. Select the profile for your actual printer.
Bambu Studio A1 mini / Generic PLA estimates about 17 minutes with startup.

After cooling, separate any strings between the two halves. Hold the solid
cheeks close to the joint and gently lift the narrower flap from the bed
plane. It should swing upward through 180 degrees over the wider flap.
The dashboard fold slider illustrates that path. Reverse motion has less
travel because of the thickness of the flaps.

Check that the joint releases, reaches the folded position, stays captured
when gently pulled, and moves repeatedly without cracking or heavy wobble.
If it is fused, change gap from 0.40 to 0.50 in the SCAD file, export the
test again, and repeat. Avoid forcing a stuck joint.

The two moving bodies are closed, watertight meshes. Interference checks
at one-degree intervals found zero overlap through the intended upward
0–180 degree fold. Bambu Studio sliced it with supports disabled. These
are digital checks. Physical release, retention strength and cycle life
are unverified.

Once the joint works on your printer, its dimensions and clearances can be
carried into the three-screen case. Board retaining tabs will remain flexible,
and the USB edge will remain accessible. The choice of spine offsets and
fold directions for the thick three-board assembly follows this trial.

The updated local dashboard is in Folio Dashboard/index.html in the ZIP.
It includes orbit, fold, exploded separation and movable solid cross-sections.
Use Inside the cones to see the two local cone bearings along their axis.
