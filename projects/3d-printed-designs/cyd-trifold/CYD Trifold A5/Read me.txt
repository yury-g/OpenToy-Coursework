A5 — COMPACT ONE-PRINT CYD TRIFOLD
2026-11-24

Print the complete chassis as ONE object, keeping its THREE captive bodies in
the supplied positions. No separate hinge pins, cartridges, panels or bezels.
Install only the boards after printing and releasing the joints.

The four hinge units now sit along the boards, with their 3 mm feet beneath
the PCBs and their bearing supports in the side seams. Unit centers are
Y +/-30.5 mm; they extend to Y +/-43.5 mm, just 0.5 mm past the 86 mm board.
The frame itself is 99 mm long, including board catches and USB-end lips.
This replaces the 160 mm A4 frame. The rounded hinge crowns remain visible
above the screen plane: this is an integrated back frame, not a flush hidden
hinge or a closed protective enclosure. Board edges remain exposed.

A HINGE AND STRUCTURE
The preferred A opposed-cone/divot contacts retain radius 3.35 mm, depth
2.85 mm, normal clearance 0.40 mm and axial clearance 0.50 mm. The bed-rooted
supports are taller than the original study; non-bearing cheek edges are
narrowed to fit the seams and support windows remove low-load material.
There are two aligned short units per seam, four total.

The load paths use 3 mm-thick bases and hinge feet, a 6 mm center spine,
8 mm cross ribs, 3 mm bearing-support posts/walls, broad PCB corner seats
and protected catch roots. Original 3.5 mm perimeter ribs are locally
relieved for the rotating bearings; the narrow remaining bay lips are
backed by broad hinge feet. The left outside rim has a separate integral
3 mm-wide low reinforcing rib. Short floor-level pads anchor the catch
guards without crossing the moving flexures.

CAD volume is 61.24 cm3, versus 80.01 cm3 for this same design with
relief windows disabled: 23.46% removed. At solid PLA density 1.24 g/cm3,
the solid-equivalent mass is 75.94 g. Actual slicer mass depends on infill,
walls and the selected profile. No physical strength or fatigue test has
been performed on this adaptation.

PRINT FLAT
Use the arranged A5 plate 3MF, or One-piece trifold - print flat.stl at 100%
scale in its supplied flat orientation. The individual left/middle/right
meshes are inspection assets; do not separately print and assemble them.
The three shells all contact the bed. No elevated downward mesh facets
exceed 45 degrees from vertical, within numerical tolerance. Keep supports
and raft off, and any brim clear of the moving gaps. Inspect the sliced
bearing clearances and horizontal flexures. Use the actual installed nozzle;
no printer profile or hardware setting was changed. The existing A clearance
assumptions came from 0.4 mm nozzle / 0.20 mm layer design work.

BOARD INSTALLATION
For the confirmed 86 x 50 mm CYDs with both USB-C and micro-USB: tuck the USB
end's bare PCB corners under the fixed lips. Hold the opposite, protected
catches back slightly, lower the other end onto all four seats, then release.
The checked insertion tilt is 0–10 degrees about the USB end. Push bare PCB
corners, not glass. Reverse this sequence for removal; do not force a binding
catch. The full 50 x 69 mm glass envelope is accounted for.

Each catch is 5.2 mm wide and carried by a horizontal 2 x 3 mm beam with about
17 mm effective free span. Rear stops limit nominal travel to 0.9 mm; side
guards have 0.8 mm clearance. Cutting the two beam roots digitally separates
exactly two catch/beam bodies from the frame, confirming no unintended fusion.
The released-head and insertion checks use rigid positions, not an elastic
force simulation. Spring force, fit and print release require a trial.

PORTS AND BUTTONS
Both USB approaches on each board were checked using separate 18 x 11 mm plug
envelopes with 22 mm insertion depth at X offsets +/-10 mm, followed by a
6 x 6 mm cable corridor continuing beyond the frame. These are conservative
design proxies, not measurements of this exact dual-USB board revision.
The short end remains open for USB and the adjacent power connection.

For inner-edge GPIO/extended IO, DHT, speaker and microSD access, use the
service pose: left +90 degrees, right -90 degrees, middle fixed. This clears
the opposite bearing support from the insertion paths. The route to that
pose was checked with loaded boards. DHT's nominal nearest cheek margin is
only 0.5 mm, so actual plug sizes and connector positions still need checking.
Do not fold with protruding cables, cards or added wiring in the sweep.

Each board has an 8.4 mm rear button-access opening. Approximate photo-derived
RESET/BOOT centers were checked with separate 3 mm blunt-probe paths; this is
not a measured fingertip-access claim. Component and port placements remain approximate envelopes.

FOLDING
Start flat, screens facing you. Fold LEFT over the middle, then RIGHT over the
pair. Unfold in reverse order. Left axis X=-32, Z18.3 rotates +180 degrees;
right axis X=32, Z28.3 rotates -180 degrees. Both axes run along Y. This is an
inward roll fold. No detent, closing latch or friction-holding function is
claimed. The 64 mm pitch gives 6 mm nominal tray-edge seams and 20.8 mm gaps
between the 43.2 mm active display widths; the three panels are distinct LCDs.
Open: 186 x 99 x 32.65 mm. Closed: 72.8 x 99 x 56.6 mm.

CHECKS
CAD verification.json and CAD verification log.txt include three watertight
bodies, 362 staged fold poses, cross-panel PCB/glass/rear-envelope tests,
six USB plug/cable corridors, 21 service and probe checks, service-pose travel,
latch isolation/travel, released-catch insertion, actual mesh layer sections,
